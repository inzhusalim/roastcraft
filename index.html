A few things to know

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Your subscription should be as simple as a good cup of coffee. Here’s what you need to know.

Talk to our team
What is specialty coffee?

It’s coffee chosen for its traceable origins and distinctive flavor. We source 100% Arabica scoring 84 points or higher, taste every batch, and roast each origin to bring out its natural character.

How often will my coffee arrive?

One box each month. We roast fresh batches every week, so your coffee ships fresh. Your box contains one, two, or four 8.8 oz (250 g) bags, depending on your plan. Shipping is free throughout the contiguous United States.

Can I order ground coffee?

Absolutely. Choose a grind for espresso, pour-over, Turkish coffee, or French press at checkout. Have a grinder at home? Select whole bean. Grinding is always included.

How does annual billing work?

Pay for 12 months upfront and save 15%. We still send one box every month. Each plan shows the monthly equivalent and the total annual charge, so you know exactly what to expect.

Can I pause my subscription?

Yes. Pausing moves your next shipment and keeps any prepaid boxes on your annual plan. You can also switch your coffee or grind before the next roast. Contact our team to make changes.

What if my coffee isn’t the right match?

Tell us what you liked and what you’d change: more sweetness, more body, or less acidity. We’ll help fine-tune your grind, recipe, and next coffee choice.
